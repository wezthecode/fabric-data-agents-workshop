# 2. Prep for AI

[Workshop home](../README.md) · Back: [1. Semantic model](01-semantic-model.md) · Next: [3. Build two ways](03-build-two-ways.md)

Prep for AI is where the semantic model tells AI what things mean. Data agents over a semantic model read it; they don't read their own instructions for DAX.

1. In the workspace, open `sm_contoso_cycles` (the model item) → **Prep data for AI** on the ribbon.
2. If the tabs are greyed out, select **Turn on Q&A**. (Q&A experiences are being retired in February 2027; today Prep for AI still needs it.)
3. Look at the three tabs:
   - **Simplify data schema:** which tables, columns and measures AI should use. Tick all six tables and the three measures → **Apply**.
   - **Verified answers:** a report visual you've approved for a question, with trigger phrases.
   - **Add AI instructions:** business terms and rules. **Leave this empty until module 4.**

## ✅ Checkpoint 2

Prep data for AI opens with all three tabs enabled, and the schema has six tables and three measures selected.
